ABOUT CG TECHNOLOGY

Straightforward tools built to make computers easier to manage

We create clean, lightweight Windows tools that eliminate headaches for everyday users, office teams, and IT professionals alike.

Why We Build These Tools

Most PC maintenance programs today are bloated, push annoying ads, or require long and confusing installation steps. We believe software should be fast, safe, and easy for anyone to understand.

Whether you are an everyday computer user trying to figure out why your PC is acting up, or a support technician managing an entire office of computers, our tools give you quick answers and one-click fixes without slowing down your machine.

Instant & Portable

Single-file downloads that run immediately. No installers to click through, no extra downloads needed, and zero background bloatware.

Simple for Users, Deep for Techs

Clean, friendly buttons for everyday fixes, with comprehensive diagnostic details and automated commands for technicians.

Safe & Transparent

Every check and repair tells you exactly what it is doing. No mystery background tasks, and no unapproved changes to your system.
